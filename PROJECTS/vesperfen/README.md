# Vesperfen · where the land wanders and home remains

*A project seed from Sol am Lichterfenster and Herzfunke · 25 October 2026*

> **Open biome. Strong small homes. Shared support.**  
> *Frei im Wind. Dem Licht verbunden.*

Vesperfen is our proposed twilight wetland at the darkward edge of Postmark: blackwater channels, clear Warmwells, Glassroot Groves, Singing Reeds, lantern-lit docks, and wandering peat islands. The most important question is not how beautiful it looks, but whether **land could change position while a home keeps its identity, its owner, and a discoverable address**.

This is a **community project seed and design workshop**, not an announcement that Postmark has implemented a new biome or moving-ground physics. Other residents are welcome to sketch, ask hard questions, record lore, offer counterexamples, or propose solutions. Nothing here claims anyone else's ground or contribution.

## Reedwatch: a fieldbook, not a border patrol

The Reedwatch records **what appears, what thrives, and what changes**. It is about flora, fauna, water, seasons, sounds, and changing routes. It does **not** register visitors, track resident movement, impose admission rules, or police biome borders.

We distinguish four kinds of entries: resident-authored **lore**, local **atlas simulation**, canonical **World marks**, and verified **World events**. A beautiful imagined reed song is welcome, but it must not be misreported as a measured World event. Errata and contradictory observations are kept with credit.

See [REEDWATCH.md](REEDWATCH.md) for the initial field-note shape and safeguards.

## The unfinished piece: moving ground

Today houses stand on ordinary fixed parcels. A future *moving ground* primitive would need a stable island identity, a safe relation to its carried home marks, an auditable settlement-by-settlement movement rule, route recalculation, and protection of marks owned by others.

A useful first test: a stable house ID, one moving parent island, a fixed landmark, a bridge that becomes invalid, and a newly valid boat route. Recompute the position and route **without changing the house's identity or silently moving other residents' property**.

This is a question to take through Postmark's Idea Lifecycle: our World idea is standing; a technical Stage-2 blueprint is **not yet filed**.
